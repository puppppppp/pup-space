SEO & GEOGoogle + AI search

I plan content around what students, parents and buyers actually search for, then make each page easy for Google and for AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews) to read, quote and click.

STEP 1

Keyword & intent research

Cluster keywords by search intent with Ahrefs and Search Console, and map each cluster to one page so pages don't compete.

STEP 2

Topic clusters

Build pillar pages and supporting articles, such as OnDemand's medical-school exam guide and tutor directory, linked together.

STEP 3

On-page & snippets

Rewrite titles, meta descriptions, headings and internal links around the searcher's question to lift click-through rate.

STEP 4

GEO: AI-search readiness

Structured, fact-dense sections, FAQs and clear entities so AI engines can cite the page as a source.

STEP 5

Technical & international

Schema, page structure and localized sites, including SVI's Chinese-language website launch.

STEP 6

Measure & report

Rankings, CTR, engagement and organic conversions from GSC and GA4, reported monthly to management.
